NOTE ON PROVENANCE (added during CSF revision, August 2026)
---------------------------------------------------------------
An earlier version of Melnikov_Observability.m did not integrate
eq. (14) at all. It hard-coded the six (eps, sigma1, sigma2) pairs
below as literals and fit a cubic spline through them to produce
the console table, threshold estimate (eps_dagger = 0.1180), and
all four figures:

  eps_paper = [0.00, 0.04, 0.08, 0.12, 0.18, 0.25]
  s1_paper  = [0.198, 0.165, 0.121, 0.047, 0.009, 0.000]
  s2_paper  = [0.142, 0.108, 0.058, 0.003, 0.000, 0.000]

No source for these six numbers was found in this repository or
elsewhere. When eq. (14), as printed in the manuscript, is
integrated directly -- with the algebraic constraints on x3, x4
solved independently at every step rather than assumed -- it does
not reproduce them; it instead gives the values recorded above,
confirmed independently in MATLAB R2026a and Python/SciPy to four
decimal places. This file, and Melnikov_Observability.m, have been
corrected accordingly. See Section9_Discrepancy_Summary in the
manuscript's supplementary materials for the full account.
